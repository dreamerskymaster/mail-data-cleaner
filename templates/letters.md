# Deletion-request letters

Placeholders: `{Company}` `{address}` (the sending address, which is the only identifier)
`{name}` `{state}` `{law}`. Pick the letter with `references/legal.md`. Show the user
the final text once before sending.

## A. US state privacy law (e.g. CT, VA, CO, TX, OR; CA: swap in CCPA/CPRA)

Subject: Consumer Data Request - Deletion and Opt-Out

To the Privacy Team at {Company},

I am a {state} resident. Under the {law} and your own privacy policy, I request that you:

1. Delete all personal data you hold about me, whether I provided it or you obtained it about me.
2. Opt me out of the sale of my personal data, targeted advertising, and profiling.
3. Stop all marketing communications to this address.
4. Pass this request on to any processors or third parties you disclosed my data to, where the law requires it.

Identifier: the email address this message is sent from ({address}).

Please confirm in writing when the request is complete, within the 45 days the law
allows. If you decline any part of it, please tell me why and how to appeal. If you
keep any record because a law requires it, please say what you kept and under which law.

Regards,
{name}

## B. Financial firms exempt from state law (GLBA)

Subject: Request to Delete Personal Data and Opt Out of Marketing

To the Privacy Team at {Company},

Under your privacy policy, I request that you delete the personal data associated with
this email address ({address}), close any associated account, and stop all marketing
communications. I understand you may need to keep some records under financial-services
laws (for example, anti-money-laundering or Gramm-Leach-Bliley rules). Please keep only
what those laws require, and tell me what you kept and why.

Please confirm in writing when this is complete.

Regards,
{name}

## C. Indian companies (to the Grievance Officer)

Subject: Withdrawal of Consent and Request for Erasure of Personal Data

To the Grievance Officer, {Company},

I am withdrawing my consent to your processing of my personal data. I request that you:

1. Erase the personal data associated with this email address ({address}), and any linked account, except data you must legally keep.
2. Stop all marketing communications by email, SMS, WhatsApp and phone.

I make this request under Rule 5(7) of the Information Technology (Reasonable Security
Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and
under your privacy policy. Please also treat it as a continuing request under Sections
6(4) and 12 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, once those provisions
take effect.

Please confirm in writing within one month, as Rule 5(9) requires. If you keep any data
because a law requires it, please say what you kept and under which law.

Regards,
{name}

## D. EU / UK companies (GDPR)

Subject: Erasure Request under Article 17 GDPR

To the Data Protection Officer, {Company},

Under Article 17 (erasure) and Article 21 (objection to direct marketing) of the GDPR
(the UK GDPR, if it applies to you), I request that you erase all personal data
associated with this email address ({address}) and stop all marketing. Please confirm
within one month, as Article 12(3) requires.

Regards,
{name}
